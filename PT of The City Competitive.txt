# PT of The City Competitive Intelligence Platform

## Project Requirements & Functional Scope

### Project Background

The CTO, Mr. Mohamed, requested the development of a Competitive Intelligence solution for PT of The City to provide visibility into the competitive landscape surrounding PT of The City locations across New York City.

The goal was to create a centralized platform capable of identifying competitors, collecting business intelligence, analyzing market conditions, and visualizing insights to support strategic decision-making.

The platform is intended to assist leadership, operations teams, marketing teams, and business development teams in understanding competitor activity, market saturation, reputation trends, service offerings, insurance coverage, and expansion opportunities.

---

# Business Objectives

The project was designed to answer the following business questions:

* Who are the primary competitors near each PT of The City location?
* Which competitors represent the greatest threat?
* What services and specialties are competitors offering?
* Which insurance providers are accepted by competitors?
* How do competitors compare to PT of The City?
* What are the strengths and weaknesses of nearby competitors?
* What are the strengths and weaknesses of PT of The City compared to competitors?
* Which neighborhoods have high or low competitive density?
* What patient feedback trends exist across competitors?
* Where should PT of The City focus future growth and expansion?

---

# Solution Overview

The solution consists of five primary components:

### 1. Competitor Data Collection & Scraping Engine

Automated collection of competitor data from multiple public sources.

### 2. Data Enrichment & Competitive Intelligence Engine

AI-powered enrichment and scoring of competitor information.

### 3. Executive Dashboard

Business intelligence dashboard for leadership and operational teams.

### 4. Interactive Market Map

Geographic visualization of PT of The City and competitor locations.

### 5. Competitor Profile & Analytics Pages

Detailed competitor-level intelligence and benchmarking.

# Data Collection & Scraping Requirements

## Competitor Identification

### Data Sources

* Google Maps
* Google Business Profiles
* Yelp
* Official Clinic Websites
* Social Media Profiles
* Public Insurance Directories
* Public Healthcare Listings

### Search Methodology

For each PT of The City location:

1. Search within a configurable radius (default: 3 miles).
2. Identify all physical therapy competitors.
3. Remove duplicate clinics found within overlapping search areas.
4. Classify clinics as:

   * PT of The City Locations
   * Competitor Locations
